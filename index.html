The Story

Made stitch by stitch, with every dog in mind.

Dapper Paws started with a foster dog, a sewing machine, and the conviction that a well-dressed pup is a happy pup. Every tie is cut, sewn, and finished by hand — in fabrics chosen because they're as joyful as the dogs who wear them. We're a one-person shop, and we love it that way.
